Privacy Policy

How Avion Partners Limited collects, uses and protects personal data, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Avion Partners Limited (RC 1938527) ("Avion", "we", "us") is a consulting company based at 3rd Floor, Coscharis Motor Plaza, Plot 388 Constitution Avenue, Central Business District, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria. We are registered with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) as a data controller/processor.

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit this website, contact us, or use our services. It is written to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and related NDPC regulations.

2. Personal data we collect

When you contact us

This website has no contact form. If you call or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, organisation, phone number, email address and information about your project or enquiry.

When we work with you

When you become a client or supplier, we process the business contact details and project information needed to deliver and manage the engagement, issue invoices and meet our legal obligations.

Identity enrolment (NIMC Front-End Partner)

As an approved Front-End Partner of the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC), we collect the demographic and biometric data (such as fingerprints and photographs) needed for National Identification Number (NIN) enrolment. We collect this data only for enrolment, on behalf of NIMC and in line with NIMC's regulations, and submit it to NIMC's systems. We do not use enrolment data for any other purpose.

When you visit this website

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and this website does not set cookies of its own. However:

  • Our web host automatically records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit) to keep the site secure and working.
  • Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google.
  • Our Contact page shows an embedded Google Map. Google may set cookies and collect data when the map loads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Why we use your data, and our lawful basis

  • To answer your enquiries: on the basis of your consent, or our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • To provide our services and manage contracts: because processing is necessary to perform a contract with you or to take steps you request before entering into one.
  • To carry out NIN enrolment: to comply with legal obligations and NIMC requirements, and in the public interest.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations: for example record-keeping and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To keep this website secure: on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting our systems.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Who we share your data with

We share personal data only when necessary, with:

  • the National Identity Management Commission, for NIN enrolment;
  • service providers who support our business, such as our website host and email provider, under appropriate confidentiality and data protection terms;
  • professional advisers, regulators, courts or law enforcement agencies, where the law requires it or to establish or defend legal claims.

5. International transfers

Some service providers, such as Google, may process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards permitted under Part VIII of the NDPA, such as adequacy, contractual protections or your consent.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including any period required by law (for example, tax and company records). When it is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

7. How we protect your data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including access controls, secure systems and staff confidentiality obligations. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the NDPC and, where required, you, as the NDPA requires.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used, and to access a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted, in certain circumstances;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a commonly used format, and have it transferred to another organisation where applicable;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this does not affect processing already carried out).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@avion.com.ng with the subject "Data protection request". We may need to confirm your identity, and we will respond within the time the law allows.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

9. Children

This website and our consulting services are not directed at children. Any children's data processed during NIN enrolment is collected with the consent and presence of a parent or guardian, as NIMC requires.

10. Links to other websites

This website links to other sites, such as LinkedIn and Google Maps. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
