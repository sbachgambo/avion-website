Our services are shaped around the sectors we know best.

Government Agencies & Public Sector

Avion's compliance with BPP, PENCOM and NSITF, and our experience in government contracts (e.g. SMEDAN's MSME database and NBRRI consultancy), make us ideal for public-sector projects and pre-shipment inspection.

Private Sector Corporations

Our expertise in project management, M&E and general contracts suits businesses scaling their operations or needing compliance support.
