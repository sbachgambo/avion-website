07 — New service

Identity Enrolment & NIMC Services

NIN enrolment as an approved Front-End Partner of the National Identity Management Commission.

Avion Partners is an approved Front-End Partner (FEP) of the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC). We extend NIMC's enrolment network by registering individuals for the National Identification Number (NIN), and we organise enrolment exercises for organisations and communities.

  • NIN enrolment for individuals
  • Organised enrolment drives for employers, schools and institutions
  • Community enrolment outreach
  • Guidance on identity enrolment requirements
